Commander Deck Reviewer

See what your deck does well, find the gaps, and get practical upgrade ideas. Built for strategy, not just a pile of card counts.

Bring your deck to the table

Import a public Moxfield deck when the server bridge is connected, or paste an exported decklist for a review right now.

Private or unlisted decks may not be accessible. Automatic link import requires a Cloudflare Worker endpoint; the browser cannot safely assume Moxfield will allow direct requests.
Link import is not connected yet. For a working review today, open your Moxfield deck, export/copy its decklist, then use the Paste decklist tab. The page is prepared for a Worker endpoint to be added later.
Accepts common exports such as “1 Sol Ring” or “Sol Ring”. Put the commander on its own line. Section headings and blank lines are ignored.
What this first version checks: deck size, duplicate names, commander/colour identity where card data is available, land count, mana curve, ramp, draw, removal, board wipes, protection and potential gaps. Suggestions are heuristic starting points, not a promise of tournament-level deck tuning.
